## Design Context

### Users

A user uses this private local dashboard to monitor personal finances, analyze monthly spending, retag transactions, and track savings alongside an optional shared joint relationship account.

### Brand Personality

Classy, luxurious, modern, and vibrant. An executive personal finance experience with airy whitespace, refined typography, and high visual clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme**: Crisp white background with subtle ambient color blooms and frosted glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).
- **Accents**: Bold, strong vibrant gradients (electric cyan/blue mesh, warm sunset coral, vivid violet, emerald green) inspired by high-end modern interfaces.
- **Typography**: Refined, elegant sans-serif typography (Plus Jakarta Sans) with light to medium weights (400–600). Strictly no brutish 800+ extra-bold type.
- **Colors**: Vibrant category colors (no washed-out pastels). Non-stressful financial colors: expenses in calm, legible dark ink (`#0f172a`), credits/income in vibrant emerald (`#10b981`).
- **Icons**: Custom SVG category icons in vibrant squircle badges for instant glanceability across Explore, Relationship, and Dashboard.
- **Microcopy**: Every letter earns its place. Zero boilerplate or repetitive labels. Clean headers and direct metrics.
